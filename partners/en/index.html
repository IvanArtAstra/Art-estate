How it works

What we do

  • List your properties in our catalogue with your photos and prices
  • Answer the first questions and establish budget, timeline and what the buyer actually needs
  • Hand you a prepared client, not a name from a web form
  • Stay with the buyer in your language and in ours

What we need from you

  • A property feed: XML, a spreadsheet, or access to your system
  • Photos free of third-party watermarks, and the right to publish them
  • Current prices and availability, refreshed at least weekly
  • Written commission terms and who handles the transaction

Money

Commission on closed deals only. We ask for nothing upfront, charge no listing fee and do not sell leads. The rate and payment terms are agreed in writing before the first property goes live.

Where local law requires a licensed agent to handle the transaction, we act as the introducing party and the paperwork stays with you. That is a normal arrangement and it works for us.

About data. We do not copy other people’s catalogues and we do not publish photos carrying someone else’s watermark. If a listing did not come from the rights holder, it does not go on our site.
